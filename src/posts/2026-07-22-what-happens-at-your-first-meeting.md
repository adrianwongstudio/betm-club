Your first visit is easier than most people expect. You arrive, take a seat, and watch the meeting unfold. There is no obligation to speak, and there is no cost.

## The opening

The Chairperson welcomes the room, introduces themselves, and hands off to the various meeting roles: the Timer, the Ah-Counter, the Grammarian, and the General Evaluator. Each has one job, and each explains it in about thirty seconds.

## Prepared speeches

Two or three members deliver prepared speeches from the Pathways learning path. Speeches run five to seven minutes, and every one gets both a written and a spoken evaluation.

## Table Topics

Then the room opens up for Table Topics — one-minute impromptu answers to a question you have not seen. Guests are welcome to try, but nobody is called on without asking first.

## Evaluations and the close

The evaluators share what worked and what to try next, the Timer reports the numbers, and the meeting closes on time. Stay for a few minutes after — that is when the real introductions happen.
